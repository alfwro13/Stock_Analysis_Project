Mean Reversion Screener

Find stocks fundamentally in a long-term uptrend (>200D SMA) experiencing a severe short-term panic sell-off.

← Back to Reports

How to use: This is a "bargain hunter" tool. It finds stocks that are strong long-term but have suddenly "snapped" like a rubber band due to short-term panic.
When to use: Use this when you want to buy a high-quality stock "on sale" during a temporary dip.
The Benefit: It prevents you from "chasing" high prices — instead it identifies a low-risk entry point where the price is likely to "snap back."

Key Metrics Explained:
  • 200D SMA: The ultimate baseline for long-term institutional health. We only screen for stocks above this line to ensure we aren't catching a "falling knife."
  • Dist from 200D (%): Shows how far the stock has pulled back toward its long-term baseline. A smaller positive number means it is closer to major institutional support.
  • Maximum RSI Threshold: A value of 30 or below is mathematically "oversold." Setting this threshold to 30 strictly isolates stocks experiencing extreme, irrational short-term fear.
