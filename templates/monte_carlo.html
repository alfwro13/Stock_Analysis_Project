1. Set Your Portfolio Value

The Portfolio Value field is pre-filled from your latest X-ray snapshot. Override it with any starting lump-sum. Set a Monthly Contribution to model regular additional investments.

2. Choose Your Horizon & Target

Select a 10, 20, or 30-year projection window. Enter an optional Target Wealth to see the probability that any simulated path reaches or exceeds that value by the end of the horizon.

3. Adjust Drift & Inflation

The Annual Drift inputs set your expected nominal return per asset class. The engine weights these by your portfolio's actual holdings. Raise or lower them to stress-test optimistic or pessimistic scenarios. Set Inflation to see real purchasing-power projections alongside nominal ones.

4. Read the Fan Chart

The blue band is the nominal (P5–P95) range; the blue solid line is the median nominal path. The red dotted line is the median real path (inflation-adjusted). The narrower the fan at a given year, the more predictable the outcome. The Probability of Success card shows how many of the 1,000 paths hit your target.
