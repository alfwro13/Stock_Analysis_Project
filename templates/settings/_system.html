📈 Pairs Spread Monitor

Scans your portfolio and watchlist (same-currency pairs only) for tickers that already show a strong rolling correlation, then flags pairs whose price log-spread has diverged from its own trailing-year mean by more than the z-score threshold below — a mean-reversion signal distinct from any single-ticker alert in this app. This schedule covers the Portfolio + Watchlist scope only — a full market Universe scan is on-demand only, triggered from the page itself. Results appear at the Pairs Spread Monitor report.

How to read this table

Each job has a non-indented status row that routes that job's start / success / error messages. Indented beneath it (marked ↳) are the individual alerts that job fires; each routes independently. For example, the checkboxes on Crash & Moonshot Alerts control the orchestrator job's status messages, while the ↳ Crash Alert and ↳ Moonshot Alert rows control those specific alerts. A status row and its alert can share a near-identical name (e.g. Portfolio Earnings Alerts vs. ↳ Portfolio Earnings Alert) — that is expected, not a duplicate.

The Fear & Greed chart sends a file attachment to Nextcloud Talk through its own enable toggle (in its own Settings panel). Its row here routes only its log / in-app status messages — not the attachment itself.
