How to read this table

Each job has a non-indented status row that routes that job's start / success / error messages. Indented beneath it (marked ↳) are the individual alerts that job fires; each routes independently. For example, the checkboxes on Crash & Moonshot Alerts control the orchestrator job's status messages, while the ↳ Crash Alert and ↳ Moonshot Alert rows control those specific alerts. A status row and its alert can share a near-identical name (e.g. Portfolio Earnings Alerts vs. ↳ Portfolio Earnings Alert) — that is expected, not a duplicate.

The morning & lunchtime briefings and the Fear & Greed chart send file attachments to Nextcloud Talk through their own enable toggles (in their own Settings panels). Their row here routes only their log / in-app status messages — not the attachment itself.
