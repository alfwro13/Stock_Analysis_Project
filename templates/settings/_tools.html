🏠 Bubble Radar

Scans your portfolio and watchlist for tickers exhibiting valuation euphoria across seven metrics: SMA-200 Extension, RSI high, P/S, PEG, FCF Yield vs Real 10Y, IV Call Skew, and SPY vs RSP Breadth. Results appear on each flagged stock's detail page and at the Bubble Radar tools page.

📈 Pairs Spread Monitor

Scans your portfolio and watchlist (same-currency pairs only) for tickers that already show a strong rolling correlation, then flags pairs whose price log-spread has diverged from its own trailing-year mean by more than the z-score threshold below — a mean-reversion signal distinct from any single-ticker alert in this app. This schedule covers the Portfolio + Watchlist scope only — a full market Universe scan is on-demand only, triggered from the page itself. Results appear at the Pairs Spread Monitor report.

🧭 Sector-Relative Momentum

Ranks every stock in the market universe against its sector peers over the last 63 and 126 trading sessions. It reads the price history and sector data that the Quantamental Analysis Engine has already cached, so set the time after that job finishes. Results appear at the Sector-Relative Momentum report, in the optional Portfolio and Watchlist columns, and in the AI prompt's sector comparison.

📋 Stable Shortlist

Once a week, turns the nightly ML Upside and Quant Score rankings into two slow-moving shortlists (the ML Upside Shortlist and Quant Score Shortlist tabs on Predicted Movers), one for your Portfolio and one for your Watchlist. Only a few names are swapped per snapshot, so the lists change far less than the live leaderboard. It reads the ML predictions and quant scores that are already stored, so set the time after the Quantamental Analysis Engine has finished. Results appear on the Predicted Movers page and in the optional Portfolio and Watchlist columns.

🔍 Forensic Screener

Monthly forensic accounting analysis for your portfolio and watchlist. Runs two jobs on the 1st of each month: first downloads annual financial statements from Yahoo Finance, then computes Piotroski F-Score, Altman Z-Score, and Beneish M-Score. Alerts are dispatched via the Notification Router when distress thresholds are breached. Full results at the Forensic Screener tools page.
