⚖️ Alert Confidence Referee

Pilot meta-labeling veto for the Market Trap & Recovery Monitor: a secondary classifier trained on Trap Monitor's own historical hit/miss record that can suppress a new alert when it resembles past false positives. Distinct from the Alert Dedup cooldown above — that gate stops the same condition re-firing; this stops a first-time low-quality signal from firing at all.
