📖 How it Works

This tool uses risk-parity to calculate exactly how many shares you should buy so you never lose more than your strict risk budget.

First, it calculates your maximum allowed loss in cash based on your settings (e.g., risking 1% of a £10,000 account = £100 max loss).

Next, it looks at the stock's recent volatility envelope to place a mathematically safe stop-loss tailored to this specific asset's normal daily price swings.

It then divides your total cash risk budget by the risk-per-share (the distance from your entry price down to the stop-loss).

The final result is an institutional position size that dynamically shrinks your share count for highly volatile stocks, and expands it for slow-moving, stable ones.

{{ anomaly_chart_html | safe }}

How to read this chart: The top panel shows the Isolation Forest anomaly score (0–1) for the last 90 trading days. The score measures how statistically unusual this stock's behaviour is across six dimensions simultaneously — volume, RSI, daily return, SMA50 distance, volatility, and beta. Cyan dots indicate normal trading; red dots indicate the score has crossed the alert threshold (dotted gold line), meaning the combination of signals is rare for this specific ticker. A sustained red period does not predict direction — it signals a structural regime change that often precedes a significant price move in either direction. The bottom panel shows the closing price for context; look for correlation between anomaly spikes and subsequent price action.
