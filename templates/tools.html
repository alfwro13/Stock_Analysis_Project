Available Tools

Standalone analytical tools for deeper investigation beyond the main dashboard. Each tool runs on demand — data is fetched live when you open it.

🎯 Dip Radar Summary

Live intraday capitulation detector across all stocks you are monitoring today. Shows each ticker's reversal score (0–100), signal breakdown, and current price — all refreshed every 2 minutes.

Use during market hours to spot potential bottoming zones across your watchlist without visiting each stock individually.

🎲 Options Sandbox

Interactive options chain explorer. Fetch live calls and puts for any ticker, visualise the IV smile, and inspect open interest and volume across expiries.

Use to explore option pricing, compare expiries, and spot unusual activity before entering a position.
Launch Tool →

🇬🇧 SMGB.L Morning Price Predictor

Estimates what SMGB.L (a London-listed semiconductor ETF priced in GBP £) will open at the next UK trading morning. Tracks the actual top-10 ETF semiconductor holdings, uses post-LSE-close US intraday prices as the primary signal, and applies a GBPUSD FX adjustment. Includes a time-aligned intraday overlay chart and a 60-day OLS regression confidence interval.

Use after the US close to gauge SMGB.L's likely opening gap before the LSE opens at {{ lse_open_time }}.
Launch Tool →

🤖 AI Sector Contagion Monitor

Tracks the broader AI ecosystem — not just semiconductors but also hyperscalers, cloud platforms, and AI-adjacent names. Shows a 30-day normalised performance chart, today's intraday session, and a rolling 20-day pairwise correlation heatmap across all 10 tickers.

Use to detect sector-wide contagion — when one major AI name sells off, does the rest of the sector follow?
