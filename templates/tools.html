🇬🇧 SMGB.L Price Predictor

Estimates what SMGB.L (a London-listed semiconductor ETF priced in GBP £) will open at the next UK trading morning. Tracks the actual top-10 ETF semiconductor holdings, uses post-LSE-close US intraday prices as the primary signal, and applies a GBPUSD FX adjustment. Includes a time-aligned intraday overlay chart and a 60-day OLS regression confidence interval.

Use after the US close to gauge SMGB.L's likely opening gap before the LSE opens at {{ lse_open_time }}.

🎭 Market Trap & Recovery Monitor

Tracks the full post-crash lifecycle across your portfolio and key market proxies: Bull Trap, Bear Trap, Capitulation, and Wyckoff Accumulation. Each ticker shows its current lifecycle phase and individual signal levels.

Use after any significant market crash — to assess whether a bounce is real, a trap is forming, and when the true bottom may be in.

📈 Market Regime (HMM)

Classifies the current market into one of three HMM states — Bull (low vol, positive returns), Chop (elevated vol, indecisive), or Crash (high vol, negative returns) — by fitting a 5-year Gaussian HMM on SPY returns and EWMA volatility. Shows the full Viterbi history, empirical transition probabilities, and per-regime return/vol statistics. Alerts via Nextcloud when the regime changes.

Use to understand the macro volatility context before acting on individual stock signals — a Bull Trap in a Crash regime is far more dangerous than the same signal in a Bull regime.
Launch Tool →

📉 Historical Stress Tester

Simulates your portfolio through the GFC 2008, Dot-com 2000, COVID-19 crash, and 2022 inflation shock using beta-adjusted scenario shocks. Shows the estimated monetary loss per holding and by sector.

Use it to see a concrete £X worst-case figure before the next crash — not after.

📊 ETF Price Predictor

Generic morning price predictor for any ETF. Configure an ETF ticker and its top 10–20 constituent tickers with weights — the system predicts the ETF's next-session open using a holdings-weighted basket return engine and an OLS regression engine, with FX adjustment where needed. Tracks prediction accuracy over time.

Use to build custom predictors for any ETF you hold — configure once, get daily predictions across multiple setups from the same tool.
