🇬🇧 SMGB.L Morning Price Predictor

Estimates what SMGB.L (a London-listed semiconductor ETF priced in GBP £) will open at the next UK trading morning, based on the US semiconductor basket's evening close. Applies GBPUSD FX adjustment and uses actual ETF holdings weights where available, falling back to a 60-day OLS regression model.

Use after the US close to gauge SMGB.L's likely opening gap before the LSE opens at {{ lse_open_time }}.
