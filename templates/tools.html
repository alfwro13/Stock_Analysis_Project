Quantamental Tools

Standalone analytical tools for deeper investigation beyond the main dashboard. Each tool runs on demand — data is fetched live when you open it.

🧮 Portfolio Optimizer

Compares your current holdings against two "textbook" alternative mixes worked out purely from how these tickers have actually moved in the past: a Steadiest Mix and a Best Reward-for-Risk Mix. Also lets you tick in Watchlist tickers to see if the model thinks they're worth considering.

Use to see how your current mix compares to two reference points. Informational only — this app has no order execution, nothing rebalances automatically.
Launch Tool →

📊 Portfolio Tearsheet

A native performance tearsheet computed entirely from your portfolio's own cached return history: risk-adjusted return ratios, drawdown duration analytics, and win/loss statistics, alongside an underwater chart, a cumulative growth chart versus benchmark, and a monthly returns heatmap.

Use for a deeper historical performance read than the X-ray's headline risk cards — especially drawdown recovery time and win/loss consistency, which the X-ray doesn't show.
Launch Tool →

📈 Monte Carlo Wealth Simulator

Projects portfolio wealth over 10, 20, or 30 years using 1,000 correlated Monte Carlo paths. Shows a percentile fan (5th–95th) in both nominal and real (inflation-adjusted) terms, using live volatility and correlation data from the X-ray engine.

Use to stress-test long-term goals: set a target wealth and see the probability of reaching it given your current holdings, contribution rate, and market assumptions.
Launch Tool →

💱 FX Drag Analyzer

Decomposes each US stock position's GBP return into two components: the stock's own equity return in USD and the FX effect. Shows which positions are genuinely outperforming and which are just riding dollar strength. Covers YTD, 1-year, and 2-year windows.

Use to separate real alpha from currency illusion — especially useful after periods of significant GBP/USD movement.
Launch Tool →

📊 ETF Price Predictor

Generic morning price predictor for any ETF. Configure an ETF ticker and its top 10–20 constituent tickers with weights — the system predicts the ETF's next-session open using a holdings-weighted basket return engine and an OLS regression engine, with FX adjustment where needed. Tracks prediction accuracy over time.

Use to build custom predictors for any ETF you hold — configure once, get daily predictions across multiple setups from the same tool.
Launch Tool →

📈 Market Regime (HMM)

Classifies the current market into one of three HMM states — Bull (low vol, positive returns), Chop (elevated vol, indecisive), or Crash (high vol, negative returns) — by fitting a 5-year Gaussian HMM on SPY returns and EWMA volatility. Shows the full Viterbi history, empirical transition probabilities, and per-regime return/vol statistics. Alerts via Nextcloud when the regime changes.

Use to understand the macro volatility context before acting on individual stock signals — a Bull Trap in a Crash regime is far more dangerous than the same signal in a Bull regime.
Launch Tool →

🎭 Market Trap & Recovery Monitor

Tracks the full post-crash lifecycle across your portfolio and key market proxies: Bull Trap, Bear Trap, Capitulation, and Wyckoff Accumulation. Each ticker shows its current lifecycle phase and individual signal levels.

Use after any significant market crash — to assess whether a bounce is real, a trap is forming, and when the true bottom may be in.
Launch Tool →

📈 Pattern Detection

Scans your portfolio and watchlist for chart patterns using rolling-window swing-point detection, checking each candidate against volume confirmation, RSI divergence, and a required prior trend.

Use to spot a potential trend reversal or continuation forming on a held or watched ticker before it confirms — and to see the projected price target once it does. All detected patterns, across every covered type, show in one results panel.
Launch Tool →

🤖 AI Sector Contagion Monitor

Tracks the broader AI ecosystem — not just semiconductors but also hyperscalers, cloud platforms, and AI-adjacent names. Shows a 30-day normalised performance chart, today's intraday session, and a rolling 20-day pairwise correlation heatmap across all 10 tickers.

Use to detect sector-wide contagion — when one major AI name sells off, does the rest of the sector follow?
Launch Tool →

🎯 Dip Radar Summary

Live intraday capitulation detector across all stocks you are monitoring today. Shows each ticker's reversal score (0–100), signal breakdown, and current price — all refreshed every 2 minutes.

Use during market hours to spot potential bottoming zones across your watchlist without visiting each stock individually.
Launch Tool →

🏠 Bubble Radar

Scans your portfolio and watchlist for tickers exhibiting signs of valuation euphoria: extreme SMA-200 extension, sustained overbought RSI, stretched P/S and PEG multiples, IV call skew, and narrow market breadth. Tracks prediction accuracy over 4, 8, and 12 weeks to show how reliable the signals are historically.

Use to identify tickers that may be mathematically detached from their fundamentals — before a mean-reversion trade, before adding to a position, or as a portfolio-level euphoria check.
Launch Tool →

📉 Historical Stress Tester

Simulates your portfolio through the GFC 2008, Dot-com 2000, COVID-19 crash, and 2022 inflation shock using beta-adjusted scenario shocks. Shows the estimated monetary loss per holding and by sector.

Use it to see a concrete £X worst-case figure before the next crash — not after.
Launch Tool →

🔍 Forensic Screener

Runs institutional-grade accounting forensics on your portfolio and watchlist monthly. Computes Piotroski F-Score, Altman Z-Score, and Beneish M-Score from annual financial statements fetched via Yahoo Finance. Fires Nextcloud alerts when holdings breach distress thresholds.

Use to catch long-term holds that are quietly decaying from the inside — structural insolvency, leverage creep, and earnings quality issues that don't show up in a price chart until it's too late.
Launch Tool →

🏛️ Sovereign Debt Auction Monitor

Tracks US Treasury auction demand using data from fiscaldata.treasury.gov. Compares each auction's bid-to-cover ratio and yield tail against a 6-auction rolling baseline per maturity, with direct / indirect / dealer allocation breakdowns.

Use to detect early warning signs of rate-shock events — weak auction demand historically precedes rapid yield spikes and equity sell-offs.
Launch Tool →

🎲 Options Sandbox

Interactive options chain explorer. Fetch live calls and puts for any ticker, visualise the IV smile, and inspect open interest and volume across expiries.

Use to explore option pricing, compare expiries, and spot unusual activity before entering a position.
Launch Tool →
