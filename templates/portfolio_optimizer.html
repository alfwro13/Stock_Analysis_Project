How to read this chart:
The curve is the efficient frontier swept between and beyond the two named portfolios (markers). Points further left have lower historical volatility for their return; points on the upper-left edge of the curve are the most efficient trade-offs.

1. Choose an Account

Select which account (or the global scope) to optimize. This determines both your held tickers and their current weights.

2. Pick Candidates

Held tickers are pre-checked. Optionally tick Watchlist tickers to let the optimizer suggest bringing them in as a brand-new position (0% current weight).

3. Read the Weights

Suggested weights can be negative — this is the true unconstrained closed-form result, not a shorting recommendation. This app cannot act on it; use it as a reference point only.

4. Mind the Candidate Count

More candidate tickers relative to overlapping trading days makes the covariance estimate thinner and the weights less stable — a warning appears if the ratio gets too high.
