🧩 Portfolio Optimizer

Compares your current mix of holdings against two "textbook" alternative mixes, worked out purely from how these tickers have actually moved in the past: a Steadiest Mix and a Best Reward-for-Risk Mix. This is a reference point only — nothing here is a buy/sell instruction, and this app cannot place trades or rebalance anything automatically.

How to read this chart:

Every dot on this chart is one possible way of mixing the tickers you selected. The further right a dot sits, the bumpier that mix's year-to-year ride has been historically; the further up a dot sits, the more it would have returned per year.

The white circle is where your actual portfolio sits today. The purple curve is every mix that gave the best possible return for its level of bumpiness — no mix below-and-right of that curve makes sense, since you could always get more return for the same bumpiness (or less bumpiness for the same return) by picking a mix that's on the curve instead.

The green diamond is the single steadiest mix on the curve; the orange star is the mix that historically gave the best return for its bumpiness. If your white circle sits well below or to the right of the curve, it means — purely based on how these tickers moved in the past — a different mix of the exact same tickers could plausibly have done better. It is not advice to act on; it's a comparison point.

1. Choose an Account

Pick which account (or "Global") you want to look at. This sets which tickers you hold and how much of each.

2. Pick Your Tickers

Everything you hold is ticked already. Add a Watchlist ticker if you want to see whether the model thinks it's worth considering.

3. Read the Table

A negative number means the model would want to bet against that ticker — you can't actually do that here, so read it as "this one dragged the mix down," not as an instruction.

4. Don't Pick Too Many

The more tickers you tick, the less reliable the maths gets, since there's only so much price history to learn from. A warning appears if you've ticked too many for how much history is available.
