Quantamental Reports

Cross-ticker and cross-holding analysis — reports that look across your portfolio, watchlist, or the wider market universe, rather than at a single stock in isolation.

📈 Pairs Spread Monitor

Flags correlated pairs (same currency only) whose price log-spread has stretched more than a z-score threshold away from its own trailing-year mean. Covers your portfolio and watchlist by default, with an on-demand full market universe scan available too.

Use to spot pairs that have historically moved together but have temporarily diverged — a classic mean-reversion setup no single-ticker alert in this app can see.

🔮 Predicted Movers

Ranks tickers by ML-predicted forward price move — the 10-trading-day quantile regression band's midpoint vs current price — not actual historical movement. Covers your portfolio and watchlist by default, with a full market universe scope available too.

Use to see which of your holdings (or the wider universe) the model currently expects to move most, in either direction, over the next two trading weeks — with a linked accuracy scorecard showing how reliable those predictions have actually been.
