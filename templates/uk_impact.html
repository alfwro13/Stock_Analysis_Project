{# ── Methodology note ── #}

How this works: The Holdings model fetches SMGB.L's top constituent holdings from yfinance, computes a weighted sum of each US stock's latest daily return, then applies a GBP/USD FX adjustment. If holdings data is unavailable, it falls back to an equal-weight basket of the AI contagion engine's default tickers (NVDA, AMD, MSFT, META, GOOGL, AAPL, AVGO, SMH, SOXX, QQQ). In parallel, a 60-day OLS regression models the historical relationship between the US basket's daily return and SMGB.L's next-morning open return, providing a confidence interval. All prices are in GBP (£).
