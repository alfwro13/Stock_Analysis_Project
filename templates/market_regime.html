ⓘ How the HMM works

🟢 State 0 — Bull

Low EWMA annualised volatility. SPY returns are positive on average. The market is trending calmly upward. Institutions are accumulating. Low VIX environment.

🟠 State 1 — Chop

Elevated volatility with near-zero average returns. The market is indecisive — often corresponds to post-crash base-building, rate-uncertainty periods, or sideways consolidation. Can precede Bull or Crash transitions.

🔴 State 2 — Crash

High EWMA volatility with negative average daily returns. The market is in a sustained risk-off move. Historical examples: COVID crash (2020), 2022 bear market. The transition matrix diagonal entry shows how long these states typically persist.

⚙ Model Details

Algorithm: GaussianHMM (hmmlearn, full covariance).
Features: SPY daily log-return + 20-day EWMA annualised vol (λ=0.94).
Training: 5-year rolling window, refitted daily. State labels assigned by mean vol ascending so ordering is stable across retrains.
Caveat: HMM states are statistical constructs — treat them as a probabilistic context layer, not a trading signal.
