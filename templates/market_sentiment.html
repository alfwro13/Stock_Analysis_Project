{{ ftse_gbp_html | safe }}
Currency Impact (GBP/USD):
Inverse Correlation: Because roughly 70-75% of FTSE 100 corporate revenue is generated outside the UK (priced in USD and EUR), a weakening Pound Sterling (GBP) often drives the FTSE 100 up. Their overseas earnings are artificially inflated when converted back into GBP. Look for an inverse correlation between the GBP/USD rate and the FTSE 100 points.
