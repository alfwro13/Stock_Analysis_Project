📊 Key Metrics Explained

Momentum Indicators

Average RSI (14): Measures how fast the sector is rising or falling. Above 60 indicates the sector is running hot (bullish), while below 40 indicates heavy selling pressure.

Bullish MACD Crosses: The percentage of stocks where short-term momentum has just mathematically crossed positive, indicating a sudden influx of buying pressure.

Trend Health

Stocks > 50D SMA: The 50-day average is the institutional "health" line. High percentages here prove the sector is in a strong medium-term uptrend.

📊 Reversion Metrics

200D SMA: The ultimate institutional baseline. We only screen above this line to ensure we aren't catching a "falling knife" in a dying company.

Dist from 200D (%): Shows how far the stock has pulled back to its long-term floor. Smaller numbers are closer to major support.

RSI Threshold: A value of 30 or below is "Oversold." It isolates extreme irrational fear, which often precedes a sharp snap-back bounce.
