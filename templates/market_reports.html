Sector Trends & Momentum

How to use: Think of this as a "weather report" for the market. Instead of looking at one stock, we look at groups like Tech or Energy. When to use: Check this first before buying any stock to see if its "neighborhood" is doing well. The Benefit: It helps you "swim with the tide." It is much easier to make money picking a stock in a sector where the Average RSI is rising and most stocks are above their 50-day average.

Relative Strength Leaders

How to use: These are the "MVP" stocks—the top 50 athletes currently outperforming the market. When to use: Use this during a healthy market to find where professional investors are putting their money. The Benefit: Momentum tends to persist. By owning leaders with high RSI and positive MACD Histograms, you are holding the winners that often go up more than the average stock during bull runs.

Mean Reversion Screener

How to use: This is a "bargain hunter" tool. It finds stocks that are strong long-term (above their 200-day average) but have suddenly "snapped" like a rubber band due to short-term panic. When to use: Use this when you want to buy a high-quality stock "on sale" during a temporary dip. The Benefit: It prevents you from "chasing" high prices. Instead, it mathematically identifies a low-risk entry point where the price is likely to "snap back" to its normal level.
