🔵 Bear Trap
After a crash, a bounce on low volume is a Bull Trap (fake recovery). If it fails, watch for Capitulation (extreme volume flush) then Wyckoff base-building before the real recovery. A Bear Trap occurs when a breakdown immediately reverses — short sellers get squeezed.

🔴 Bull Trap / Dead Cat Bounce

Price bounces after a crash but volume on up-days is far lower than volume on down-days. Smart money is not buying. Often precedes a secondary sell-off.

SEVERE vol ratio < 0.75   ELEVATED < 0.90

🔵 Bear Trap / False Breakdown

Price briefly breaks below support but closes back above on low volume. Short sellers are trapped in a losing position as price reverses.

🔴 Capitulation (Final Flush)

Volume > 3σ above the 20-day mean with RSI < 30 and price deeply below the 20-EMA. Statistical signature of a potential true bottom — institutions absorbing panic selling.

🟢 Wyckoff Accumulation

Bollinger Bands squeeze to < 2% of price while ATR and volume contract. Institutions quietly absorbing supply. A volume-confirmed breakout typically follows.
