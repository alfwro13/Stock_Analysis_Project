ⓘ Signal & Phase Reference

🔴 Bull Trap / Dead Cat Bounce

Price bounces after a crash but volume on up-days is far lower than volume on down-days. The recovery lacks institutional conviction — smart money is not buying. This often precedes a secondary sell-off.

SEVERE TRAP RISK vol ratio < 0.75   ELEVATED < 0.90

🔵 Bear Trap / False Breakdown

Price briefly breaks below key support (Bollinger Lower Band or 20-day low) but closes back above on low volume. Short sellers are trapped in a position that reverses against them.

CONFIRMED low conviction break & recovery

🔴 Capitulation (Final Flush)

Volume > 3σ above the 20-day mean with RSI < 30 and price deeply below the 20-EMA. A long lower wick closing in the upper half of the day's range signals institutions absorbed the panic selling. This is the statistical signature of a potential true bottom.

Formula: Vol > μ + 3σ + RSI < 30

🟢 Wyckoff Accumulation

Bollinger Bands squeeze to historically narrow width (band width < 2% of price) while ATR contracts and volume dries up. This is Wyckoff's Spring / accumulation phase — institutions are quietly absorbing supply. A volume-confirmed breakout follows.

ACCUMULATION PHASE ATR + vol contracted
