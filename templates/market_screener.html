📊 Key Metrics Explained

Momentum & Trend

RSI (14-Day): Measures how fast a stock is rising or falling. Above 70 is "Overbought" (due for a pullback), while below 30 is "Oversold" (due for a bounce).

MACD Histogram: Measures acceleration. Green bars mean the stock is gaining speed upward; red or shrinking bars mean the trend is losing steam.

Golden Cross: A "super-signal" where the 50-day average crosses above the 200-day average, mathematically proving a shift into a major new uptrend.

Institutional Support

Volume: The total shares traded. High volume shows "Smart Money" conviction, while low volume moves are often unreliable "traps."

Volume Surge: Triggered when volume is 50% higher than the 20-day average. It highlights massive institutional buying or selling.

50D & 200D SMA: The 50D is the "Health Line" for medium-term trends. The 200D is the "Institutional Floor" used by large banks to judge long-term viability.
