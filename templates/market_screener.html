Key Metrics Explained:
  • Volume: Total shares traded today. High volume indicates "Smart Money" conviction, validating price action. Low volume moves are often "traps" lacking institutional backing.
  • RSI (14-Day): Oscillates 0-100. Values above 70 are "Overbought" (mean reversion risk); values below 30 are "Oversold" (potential accumulation zone).
  • MACD Histogram: Measures momentum velocity. Expanding green bars confirm acceleration; red or shrinking bars signal momentum decay or a trend pivot.
  • 50D SMA: The "Health Line" representing the 10-week price mean. Trading above it confirms a medium-term uptrend; falling below suggests a structural loss of strength.
  • 200D SMA: The "Institutional Floor." Banks and funds use this as the ultimate line in the sand; breaking below it often marks the shift into a primary bear market.
  • ML Conf: Machine Learning Ensemble confidence score predicting a >3% return over the next 5 days. >75% is highly actionable.
  • VaR (95%): Parametric Value at Risk. The expected maximum percentage loss over a standard holding period with 95% confidence.
  • Sentiment: VADER NLP compound score generated from the latest news headlines. Positive values indicate bullish media framing.
