How to read this data:
Bid-to-Cover: Measures overall auction demand. A reading well above the baseline indicates healthy appetite for US debt. Weak BTC means the Treasury had to offer higher yields to clear supply.
Yield Tail: High yield minus median yield in basis points. A large tail means primary dealers (who must bid) absorbed supply at above-market yields — a reliable sign of poor institutional demand.
Baseline: Rolling 6-auction mean per maturity, excluding the current auction — gives context-adjusted thresholds rather than fixed absolute levels.
Dealer %: Primary dealers are obligated to bid. A rising dealer share combined with falling indirect % signals foreign central banks stepping back from US debt.
