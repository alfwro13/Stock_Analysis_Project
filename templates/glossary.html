This living document explains the mathematical algorithms, fundamental logic, and trading strategies used to score and evaluate your portfolio.

🎯 The Multi-Regime Strategy (Scoring & Tags)

Our system operates on a "Dual-Lens" architecture. The 0-100 Score tells you if an asset is healthy to hold. The Setup Tags tell you exactly when it is mathematically optimal to buy or sell.

⚡ MACD Reversal

An early trend reversal setup. The MACD algorithm calculates when selling momentum has mathematically exhausted itself.

TRIGGER: Fast MACD crosses above the Slow Signal line while below the zero axis.

Strategy: Used for bottom-fishing. Buy the asset as the bleeding stops, before the moving averages even realize the trend has changed.

🔥 VCP Breakout (Volatility Contraction)

Pioneered by Mark Minervini. It identifies stocks that are winding up like a coiled spring. Institutional investors are quietly absorbing supply without causing the price to spike.

TRIGGER: Weekly price variance < 2.5% over 3 weeks AND average volume dries up by > 20%.

Strategy: The safest, highest-probability setup. Buy when the tag appears, expecting an explosive breakout as soon as volume returns.

👑 Market Leader

Capital flows to where it is treated best. This setup scans the Relative Strength (RS) of the stock against the S&P 500.

TRIGGER: 60-day RS linear slope is sharply positive AND RS is near a 60-day high.

Strategy: Add monthly capital to these stocks. Do not put money into laggards when market leaders are absorbing all the liquidity.

🚨 Divergence Warning (Circuit Breaker)

The ultimate risk-management alarm. It detects when the price is lying to retail investors.

TRIGGER: Price makes a new High, but the RSI momentum oscillator makes a Lower High.

Strategy: Sell or trim immediately. This indicates institutional distribution (selling) into retail buying pressure. A dump is imminent.

🕯️ Candlestick Anatomy Masterclass

The interactive charts use Candlesticks. Unlike line charts, candlesticks reveal the psychological war between buyers and sellers within a single timeframe.

The Real Body (The Block)

The thick colored block. It represents the Open and the Close of the period.

  • Green Body: The stock Closed higher than it Opened. Bulls won.
  • Red Body: The stock Closed lower than it Opened. Bears won.
The Wicks (The Shadows)

The thin lines extending above and below the body. They represent the extreme Highs and Lows reached during the battle before the price retreated.

  • Long Lower Wick: Price dropped hard, but buyers violently stepped in and drove it back up. (Highly Bullish Support)
  • Long Upper Wick: Price spiked hard, but institutional sellers aggressively dumped shares, driving it down. (Highly Bearish Resistance)
📈 Technical Analysis (Chart Momentum)
Moving Averages (5D, 10D, 21D, 50D, 200D)

A moving average smooths out daily price fluctuations to reveal the underlying trend.

  • 5D & 10D: Hyper-short-term momentum. If price is above, it is running hot.
  • 21D (The Swing Line): The primary short-term trend line.
  • 50D (The Health Line): Medium-term health. A stock is "healthy" if above this.
  • 200D (The Institutional Line): The ultimate dividing line. Large funds rarely buy below this.
IF 5D > 10D > 21D THEN: Short-term trends are perfectly aligned.
RSI (Relative Strength Index)

A momentum oscillator that measures the speed of recent price changes.

IF RSI > 70 THEN: Overbought. It has risen too fast and is likely to pull back.
IF RSI < 30 THEN: Oversold. It has dropped too fast and may bounce.
ATR Stop-Loss (Average True Range)

Calculates the average daily price swing. Stop Loss is 2x ATRs below current price.

IF Price drops below ATR Stop-Loss THEN: Normal volatility patterns are broken. Sell to manage risk.
🏢 Fundamental Valuation (Company Health)
Peter Lynch Fair Value PEG

Compares the P/E ratio to the company's actual earnings growth rate.

IF Peter Lynch PEG < 1.0 THEN: Severely undervalued relative to its growth.
IF Peter Lynch PEG > 2.0 THEN: Highly overvalued. Growth does not justify the price.
Beta (Systematic Risk)

A measure of volatility in relation to the S&P 500.

IF Beta > 1.2 THEN: Highly volatile (High Risk / Tech).
IF Beta < 0.8 THEN: Safer and less volatile (Low Risk / Utilities).
