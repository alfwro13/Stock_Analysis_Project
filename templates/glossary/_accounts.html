Built-in Accounts — What It Solves

Ghostfolio is a great source of live, auto-synced holdings, but it only ever gives you what its own importer captured — it doesn't let you hand-enter a trade, track cash that isn't tied to a position, or keep a brokerage account that you've never connected to Ghostfolio at all. Built-in Accounts (/accounts) is a native, database-backed alternative or companion: you create one or more accounts, log every Buy, Sell, Dividend, Interest, Fee, or Cash movement against them, and the app derives holdings, cash balance, and P&L from that ledger — no external service required.

Built-in accounts coexist with Ghostfolio rather than replacing it. The Portfolio page merges both sources: a ticker held in both a Ghostfolio account and a built-in account is summed into one row, with both accounts listed against it. If you import the same brokerage account into both systems, that ticker is counted twice — this is a known, documented trade-off rather than an automatic guard.

Transaction Types & Cash Tracking

Every position and cash movement is a row in the account's transaction ledger. Six types are supported: Buy and Sell (move shares and, by default, cash), Dividend and Interest (cash income, no shares), Fee (a cash deduction not tied to a trade), and Cash (a manual deposit or withdrawal). Each Buy/Sell row has an "Update Cash" toggle — leave it off if the trade happened outside the cash you're tracking in this account (for example, a transfer-in of shares with no corresponding cash movement).

Every transaction carries its own trade currency (USD, EUR, GBP, or GBp for London Stock Exchange pence-quoted stocks) plus an exchange rate that converts it into the account's base currency. If you don't supply one, it's auto-filled from a historical FX lookup for the transaction date, falling back to the live rate and then to 1.0.

Holdings, Closed Positions & Realized P&L

Holdings aren't stored — they're derived on every read by replaying an account's transactions in chronological order using an average-cost method: each Buy raises the position's average cost basis, each Sell realises a gain or loss against that average cost and reduces the remaining shares. A ticker that's been fully sold nets to zero open shares but isn't discarded — it survives in the ledger and appears under Closed Positions with its realized P&L, so your full trading history (wins and losses) stays visible even after you've exited a position.

Account Value Snapshot

The value-over-time chart on each account's detail page is fed by a nightly scheduled job that writes one row per account to account_value_history — total value, cash value, and equity value, all in the base currency. When you first create an account, a one-time backfill runs in the background against cached historical price data so the chart has a meaningful history immediately rather than starting from a single point. The job can also be triggered on demand from the Background Automation Schedulers panel in Settings.

Import from Ghostfolio

Rather than re-entering trades you've already recorded in Ghostfolio, the Accounts page offers a one-click "Import from Ghostfolio" control that pulls an account's entire Ghostfolio activity history into a built-in account — Buys, Sells, Dividends, Interest, and Fees, mapped to the matching transaction type, including activity for tickers you no longer hold (so they land correctly under Closed Positions). Re-running the import is safe: activities already imported are recognised and skipped, so nothing is duplicated.
