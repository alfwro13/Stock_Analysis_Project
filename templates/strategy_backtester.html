🧪 Strategy Backtester

Replays how a set of tickers would have fared under different ways of splitting the money, using only the past prices this app has stored. Every decision is made from information available on the day, and every trade happens at the next close, with trading costs and weight drift included. This is a fixed-current-basket test: a way to compare rules against each other, not a promise of what will happen. Nothing here is a buy/sell instruction and this app cannot place trades.

How to read this chart:

Each line is what the starting capital would have grown to under one strategy, after trading costs. The dashed white line is the benchmark. The lines start flat while the first purchase waits for the next close. A strategy that ends higher is not necessarily better: compare the drawdown chart and the Costs Paid and Turnover columns too.

1. Pick a Basket

Choose your holdings (plus any Watchlist tickers) or the latest Stable Shortlist, then one currency. The basket is today's list, so treat results as a comparison between rules, not a forecast.

2. Choose Strategies

Start with Buy-and-Hold and Rebalanced Equal Weight as the baselines. Anything more complicated needs to beat them after costs to be worth the effort.

3. Set Realistic Costs

Costs are what quietly separates a busy strategy from a calm one. Try the same run at None and at Typical to see how much trading eats.

4. Mind the Window

With about two years of history the rolling strategies get roughly one year to be tested. Prepare Extended History for around five years, including more than one kind of market.
